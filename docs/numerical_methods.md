# Numerical definitions and table conventions

The manuscript and Supplement give the model and derivations. These implementation details connect their notation to the released columns.

- `core.py` independently finds contour radii with Brent's method, constructs the Bloch-overlap collision operator, and solves by a full spectral pseudoinverse as well as time-reversal-odd projection and block elimination. The tests compare the independent formulations.
- `referee_numerics.py` constructs quadratic contour roots and numerical eigenspinors. Its `RevisionParameters` and `PreparedSystem` names are retained API names. Weights are q/(2*pi*N*abs(radial velocity)); weighted coordinates are g=sqrt(w)*ell. Rates are reversible Born rates with full momentum transfer. The projected operator has dimension 2N and no artificial relaxation shift.
- `mode_ablation.py` orthonormalizes the four ordered raw vectors `(g_A0, p_A, b_P, p_P)` twice. `final_basis_transformation.csv` records raw/orthonormal indices starting at 1 and the transform coefficients. Population displacements are time-reversal odd and physical-mirror odd; local q_y reflection is even. Equal-local-angle partner exchange is not an active symmetry.
- `final_mode_ablation.csv` stores 3,600 principal, 300 comparison and five representative rows. M1 through M4 all project the same full collision operator; M1/M2 therefore retain passive scattering-out. Their errors use the full observable as denominator. This differs from deleting the passive sector and all cross-sector edges.
- `final_invariant_subspace_residual.csv` gives spectral absolute and relative leakage, separate Frobenius diagnostics, drive miss, primal/dual residuals and the exact Hall-error identity. The four-dimensional contact space is invariant; for a massless untilted passive band its population mode decouples, leaving three driven modes. Finite-range leakage alone is not an error bound.
- `final_reduced_mode_coefficients.csv` retains zero-based fields `h0,h1,x0,x1`; these correspond to paper `j_1,j_2,x_1,x_2`. Its older `closure_residual` is Frobenius based; the manuscript's spectral rho uses the dedicated invariant table. `scalar_amplitude=x0-G`, `shape_amplitude=x1`, and `shape_stiffness` is an eliminated two-coordinate stiffness, not a collision eigenvalue.
- The scalar/shape correction uses the exact active reference and contour norm. S and T are diagnostic projections, not independent microscopic currents. The cancellation table holds a fixed absolute component budget, including both interval/disk and discrete-sign tests. Main statistics use positive eta; all-grid counts are supplementary.
- `final_leakage_spectrum.csv` contains full odd-space eigenmodes, not eigenvectors of the complement. Signed error residues and grouped leakage powers have the invariant interpretation; individual degenerate eigenvectors do not. Absolute-rate eigenvalues, drive/Hall overlaps and all four leakage-column overlaps are included.
- `final_mass_scaling.csv` uses `DIRECT_PASSIVE=H_P(0,m)`, `ACTIVE_COUPLING=H_A(eta,m)-H0`, and `PASSIVE_COUPLING=H_P(eta,m)-H_P(0,m)`. Its polarization identity is H_P=-m*v_P^2*(N_plus-N_minus)/(2*delta_P^3). Massless slopes use the m=0 population difference, not division by zero.
- The response convention is E(t)=Re(E0 exp(i omega t)), chi=-H/2 in e=hbar=1 units. DC values denote the low-frequency second-harmonic limit. The fixed-current open-circuit proxy uses the complex square of sigma_xx, not its magnitude square.

`make reproduce-core` uses the explicit `model` and grid entries in `config/study.json`. The untilted passive band and fixed valley centers are structural choices in the band constructor, also documented under `geometry`; changing those requires a scientific code change. Norm floors and tolerances are documented conventions. `config/supporting_controls.json` records the selected supporting scans.
